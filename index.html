Projects

3D Toy Castle in Fusion 360

One of my early CAD projects involved experimenting with Fusion 360 to design a 3D computer model of a toy castle. I began by dimensioning and creating the main base of the castle, then gradually built upon the structure by adding towers and other architectural features.

Throughout the design process, I explored and applied essential CAD techniques, including extrusions, cuts, and fillets, to shape the individual components and refine the overall model. This project helped me develop a stronger understanding of 3D modeling, dimensional accuracy, and how individual features can be combined to create a complete structure.

As one of my first major projects using computer-aided design, it served as an important introduction to digital product design and strengthened my confidence in using Fusion 360 to bring ideas to life.
